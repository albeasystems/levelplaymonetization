Support

Answers to common questions, or write to us directly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I connect my LevelPlay account?

Open Settings, go to the LevelPlay section and enter your Secret Key and Refresh Token from your LevelPlay account, then choose Test connection and save. Until you connect, the App shows demo data so you can look around.

Is it safe to enter my keys?

Your credentials are stored in the iPhone Keychain, on your device only. There is no server of ours in between: the App talks to LevelPlay directly from your device. You can remove them any time with Settings, Disconnect and delete credentials. Do this before uninstalling, because iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted.

The connection test fails. What should I check?

Check that the Secret Key and Refresh Token were copied completely, without spaces at the start or end, and that they belong to the same LevelPlay account. Make sure you have internet access. If it still fails, write to us and tell us the message the App shows. Never send us your keys.

Why do the numbers differ from my LevelPlay dashboard?

The App shows what LevelPlay's reporting service returns. Differences usually come from the date range or the time zone, because the service reports in UTC, or from data that LevelPlay has not finished processing. Compare the same period in both places.

Which networks and countries does it show?

Whatever appears in your LevelPlay report. The Networks tab ranks them with revenue share, eCPM, fill rate, show rate and impressions, and the Countries tab shows a map and an app by country matrix.

How do alerts work?

With Pro, you create a rule such as "eCPM drops by 20%", optionally for one app, country, network or ad type. Each rule compares the last 7 days with the 7 days before. Allow notifications when asked, and keep Background App Refresh on so iOS can check your data when it allows.

How do I export a report?

Tap the document icon at the top of the Overview to preview a PDF or CSV report and share it. Reports are part of Pro.

I bought Pro but I still see ads.

Close and reopen the App. If ads remain, go to Settings and choose Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If the problem continues, write to us.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings and choose Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. Pro is restored at no extra cost.

Can I change how ads are personalized?

Yes. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. In regions where a consent form is required, you can reopen it from Settings, Privacy options. Pro removes ads altogether.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, in portrait orientation. The App is available in English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese.

Contact us

If you did not find your answer, write to us and we will reply within 48 hours. Please include your iOS version, the App version (Settings) and, if you can, a screenshot. Do not include your Secret Key or Refresh Token.
